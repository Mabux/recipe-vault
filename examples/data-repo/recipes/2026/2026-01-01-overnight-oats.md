# Overnight Oats

Example recipe showing the English section headings: mix in the evening, eat in the morning.

## Ingredients

- 50 g rolled oats
- 150 g Greek yogurt
- 100 ml milk
- 1 tsp honey
- 80 g berries

## Instructions

1. Stir 50 g oats, 150 g yogurt, 100 ml milk and 1 tsp honey together in a jar.
2. Close the jar and refrigerate overnight (at least 8 hours).
3. Top with 80 g berries before eating.

## Notes

Keeps for up to 2 days in the fridge.
